Business Rules & System Guide

  • Rule 1 (Expiry Check): An expired batch can never be sold in the POS billing screen.
  • Rule 2 (Valid Stock Check): A sale is rejected if the requested quantity exceeds valid non-expired stock.
  • Rule 3 (FEFO Method): Stock is deducted from the batch with the earliest expiry date first.
  • Rule 4 (Role Access): Administrator manages users & reports; Manager records purchases & batches; Sales Staff handles customer billing.
